About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web application that allows you to quickly write down your ideas and keep track of them. It was built as part of the Web Foundations course to help learners practice HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea into the “New note” field on the Home page.
  2. Click “Add Note” to add it to your notes.
  3. Review your ideas under “Your notes.”

Features

  • Add notes quickly
  • View your notes in a list
  • Responsive design for mobile and desktop
